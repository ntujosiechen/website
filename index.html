Josie I Chen is an Associate Professor of Economics at National Taiwan University. She received her Ph.D. in Economics from Brown University in 2015 and taught at National Taipei University before joining NTU in 2019. Her research uses laboratory, online and field experiments to study how language, emotion, identity and social feedback shape economic decisions, from time preference and bargaining to trust, energy use and preferences for democracy.

Research Assistants 招募研究助理

The lab designs and runs economic experiments at the intersection of economics, psychology and political science. Each summer it recruits undergraduate research assistants from any department. No economics background is required. The lab looks for curiosity about human behavior, careful work, and patience with research questions.

團隊研究橫跨經濟學、心理學與政治學，透過設計實驗與蒐集資料探討人們如何做決策。每年暑期招募大學部研究助理，不限科系、不需經濟學背景，適合對人類行為好奇、樂於閱讀與討論、細心負責、對研究有耐心的同學。

2026 recruitment notice 招募公告Contact: josiechen@ntu.edu.tw

What research assistants do

  • Read the academic literature and discuss experimental designs with the team 閱讀文獻、討論實驗設計
  • Recruit participants and run data collection, including finding research sites 招募受試者、尋找研究場域、蒐集資料
  • Carry out careful, long-term statistical analysis 細緻且長期的統計分析

Questions the Lab Is Working On 團隊目前的研究問題

  • 語言框架的用字會影響儲蓄決策嗎？Does the wording of a language frame change saving decisions?
  • 說話速度會改變人對未來的感受嗎？Does speech rate change how people feel about the future?
  • 把室友分組競賽能減少用電嗎？Does putting roommates into teams reduce electricity use?
  • 當人們隱藏真實想法時，如何測量真正的民主偏好？How can we measure true preferences for democracy when people hide their views?
  • 善於讀懂他人意圖的人賺得比較多嗎？Do people who read others’ intentions well earn more?
  • 女校會讓女性更願意競爭嗎？Do women’s schools make women more willing to compete?
  • 上英文課會讓記憶變差嗎？Does taking a class in English hurt memory?
  • AI 撰寫的文字會讓謊言更有說服力嗎？Does AI-written text help people lie more convincingly?
